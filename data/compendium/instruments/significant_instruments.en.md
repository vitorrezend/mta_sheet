In story terms, certain instruments carry deeper metaphysical resonance than a mere tool. Under this optional rule, a Significant Instrument represents an item with extraordinary symbolic, emotional, or mythic weight in the narrative.

When a mage employs a Significant Instrument, the Storyteller may allow the player to draw upon Mythic Threads. This can be manifested as:
1. A -1 reduction to the casting difficulty of an appropriate Effect;
2. OR transforming an otherwise Vulgar Effect into a Coincidental Effect, provided the instrument’s mythic narrative and presentation make the phenomenon feel natural, inevitable, or plausible to Witnesses within that dramatic context.

This benefit reflects the profound truth that belief shapes reality, and when that belief is anchored in a tool of mythic proportion, Reality itself bows to the resonance of the symbol.
