Mage 20’s collection of instruments is extensive but not comprehensive. Considering that anything could be used as a tool for magick, it’s damned near impossible to list and describe each imaginable instrument. That said, a handful of additional instruments can be found below, and in the Uncanny Influence section of the How Do You DO That? sourcebook (pp. 118-119). And because several of these entries get pretty extensive, we’ve used a slightly different format header than the ones used in Mage 20 to define each tool and its potential use.
