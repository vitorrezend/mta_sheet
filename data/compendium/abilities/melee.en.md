Weapons are extensions of your own limbs, and you know how to use them with brutal efficiency. When fists and feet just aren’t enough to get the job done, this Skill literally gives you the fighting edge… or the blunt instrument, if need be. Melee grants your character a working knowledge of weaponry and its practical effects in combat. Certain groups, like gangs or martial arts fellowships, prize skill at arms; if you wanna hang with such people, this Trait is essential to your credibility… and perhaps your survival, too. Certain weapon maneuvers (like tangling someone up in a chain) may be used only with certain weapons or with weapons-based martial arts. As with other fighting skills, [the Well-Skilled Craftsman option](mta://abilities/rule_well_skilled_craftsman) may apply.
