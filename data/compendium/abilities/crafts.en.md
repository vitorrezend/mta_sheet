You know how to create and repair things with your hands. Pursuing a particular trade (and perhaps a number of other ones too), you’ve begun to master the techniques involved in carpentry, mechanics, leatherwork, metallurgy, or any other handicraft you could name.

When purchasing this Skill, you must define a specialty for your character even if you don’t have the usual four dots in the Trait; this specialty defines a type of Craft you pursue, and you get the bonus once you achieve at least four dots in that field. If you want your character to have several Crafts, they should be purchased separately. A mechanic who’s also good with ceramics, woodworking, and home repair, for example, would have several different Craft specialties to reflect her ability with each one.
