Because of its extraordinary disciplines and refinements, the Akashic Art of [Do](mta://abilities/do) is not considered a normal martial art. Characters who practice Do must have the Do Talent instead of or in addition to the [Martial Arts](mta://abilities/martial_arts) Skill. The same character could have both Abilities, having studied a lesser martial art before embarking upon the arduous path of Do. If she chooses to pursue Do, however, she may not buy any further dots in Martial Arts, as such devotion demands total commitment, without the distractions of lesser practices. For more details, see the entries for Do and Martial Arts in the Combat section of Chapter Nine, (pp. 423-430).
