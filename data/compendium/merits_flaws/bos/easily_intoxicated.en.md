Shit really fucks you up fast. The flipside of Alcohol/Drug Tolerance, above, this Flaw renders you especially susceptible to intoxication. Your Stamina rolls to resist the effects of drugs or poisons (though not disease), as described in the Drugs, Poisons, and Disease section of Mage 20, suffer a +3 modifier to the usual difficulty involved. Contrary to popular conception, this does not make you a good candidate for the Cult of Ecstasy because you don’t control the drugs – the drugs control you.
