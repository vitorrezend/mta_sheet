You are one imposing sumbitch, not so much large as apparently dangerous no matter what mood you might be in at the time. This could be as obvious as scarring or an intimidating build, or as intangible as the proverbial “air of menace.” System-wise, the Merit adds two dice to all Social-Trait rolls that involve intimidating someone; story-wise, people find you unnerving – and quite possibly attractive in a dangerous way – even when you’re not trying to be impressive.
