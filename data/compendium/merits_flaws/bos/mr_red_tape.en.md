Bureaucracy hates you. Any official complication that could go wrong will go wrong if you’re the one who’s dealing with the authorities. Essentially the opposite of the Merit: Master of Red Tape (p. 58), this Flaw adds +2 to the difficulty of any roll you make when trying to get something done through an established system, and throws stupid mistakes and delays into the works whenever you’ve got to work with any sort of bureaucracy.
