Life before Awakening remains a mystery to you. Friends? Family? Mundane existence? Whoever and whatever they were, you cannot recall. This amnesia might be related to PTSD, fugue-state dissociative Derangement (Mage 20, pp. 648-649), brain-trauma, Quiet, Social Processing (as shown in the Prelude for Mage 20), psychic assault (see Uncanny Influence in How Do You DO That?, pp. 114-136), Things Man Was Not Meant to Know, or other forms of identity disruption. Regardless of the reason, being a mage is all you can remember.

Beyond all of the obvious complications involved in near-total amnesia (lack of memories, social disorientation, legal nonexistence, a fractured sense of self, and so on), this Flaw also allows you to take up to five points in “mystery Flaws.” Your Storyteller will know what they are, but you won’t. Throughout the chronicle, she’ll spring them on you in various entertaining ways. Perhaps when all is revealed, you’ll be able to buy off this Flaw (or have it “paid off” by the Storyteller, in lieu of experience points), and then remember who you were. Whether or not you’ll like the answers, of course, remains to be seen…
