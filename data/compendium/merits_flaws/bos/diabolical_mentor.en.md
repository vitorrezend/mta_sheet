Your mentor wasn’t merely bad at his job – he turned out to be actively malevolent. That person could be an actual Nephandus, a demented Marauder, a ruthless wizard, a diehard “black hats and mirrorshades” type of Technocrat, an Infernalist sellsoul… maybe you don’t even know what your mentor really is, but it’s just plain bad.
