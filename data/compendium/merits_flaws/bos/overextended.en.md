You’ve tried to do too much, and the strain has begun to show. Even when you add Time Sphere magick to the equation, there really are only so many hours in a day, and you spend most of them just trying to keep up with various commitments. You’re on too many committees, stuck with too many reports, nurturing too many connections, maintaining too many resources for one person – even a mage – to handle. Other characters, on top of it all, have begun to undermine your ability to juggle all those plates, and folks are gunning for your position if and when you finally fuck up badly. While this Flaw best suits members of the Technocratic Union, it’s appropriate for any mage who belongs to a group that prizes involvement, most especially the Hermetic Order, Celestial Chorus, Wu Lung, and Templar Knights.
