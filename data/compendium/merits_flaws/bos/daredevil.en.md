You laugh at danger. Even when daring the Fates with near-suicidal impulsiveness, you possess an innate knack for not getting yourself perished. Add three dice to non-combat and non-magickal rolls that involve some physical act of incomprehensible stupidity (leaping from roof to roof, diving into a waterfall, jumping a motorcycle onto the back of a moving train, etc.) that would turn the average mortal into hash.
