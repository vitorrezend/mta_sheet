Interpersonal relations are not your forte. Commonly known these days as social anxiety, this Flaw adds +2 to the difficulty of your social rolls, thanks to your discomfort when dealing with other folks. If, all the gods forbid, you wind up as the center of attention (a situation you avoid whenever possible), your social rolls gain a +3 addition to their difficulty. Story-wise, this shyness comes across as avoidance, introversion, and awkwardness in many social situations even when you actually want to hang out and make a good impression.
