> **Source:** M20 Book of Secrets, Chapter One: Heroic Traits, “Adversarial Backgrounds,” p. 35

The Revised Edition Mage sourcebook Guide to the Traditions introduced an optional category of Traits called adversarial Backgrounds: inverted Background Traits that hamper the character instead of benefitting him. That sourcebook, however, was the one and only time such Traits appeared in a World of Darkness game. And so – for consistency’s sake – those adversarial Backgrounds have been either revised into Flaws in the following pages or, for adversarial Backgrounds that duplicated existing Flaws, eliminated altogether.

You can find the revised versions of those adversarial Backgrounds on the following pages:

- Apprentice: p. 81
- Blacklisted: p. 59
- Debts: p. 61
- Enemies: eliminated
- Gremlin: p. 84
- Infamy: p. 62
- Jinx: p. 88
- Notoriety: p. 65
- Rival House: p. 63
- Skeptics: eliminated
- Throwback: p. 85
- Uncanny: p. 86
- Wards: eliminated
