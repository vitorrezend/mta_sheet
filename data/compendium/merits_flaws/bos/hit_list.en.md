A rival group wants you dead. You might be a Templar who’s made enemies among the Etherites, an Etherite who crossed the line with the New World Order, and so forth. Whatever happened, it’s personal and extends to the entire group. As with the Vampire 20 Flaw: Clan Enmity, this is a deadly business, far worse than the one-point Sect Enmity, above. Social rolls when dealing with members of this group add +2 to their difficulty – if you get a chance to make such rolls at all. Whether or not the enemy group actively attacks you on sight, its members will go out of their way to make your life solitary, unpleasant, nasty, brutish, and short.
