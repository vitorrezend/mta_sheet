Magick tends to break your brain. And so, when mages or the people near them experience various traumas, those experiences often leave derangements: mental and behavioral challenges that impair the character and her relationship with the rest of the world.

Despite the crazy name, derangements don’t necessarily turn a character into a raving loony-tune. Most times, a deranged character is simply a bit off-kilter by ordinary human standards. (The word means “out of alignment.”) Still, these mental illnesses can cause profound difficulties, especially in their most extreme manifestations.

At low levels, such challenges simply become quirks; if they reach irrational degrees, though, derangements can become extraordinarily dangerous… especially when the person afflicted with a given derangement has the power to tear reality apart.
