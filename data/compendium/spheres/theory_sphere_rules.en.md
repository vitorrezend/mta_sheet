### General Sphere Rules & Metaphysics
*Part II: The Book of Common Magick (The Spheres)*

> **Source:** M20 Core Rulebook, pp. 511-512  
> **Topics:** Levels of Expertise, Pattern Spheres, Conjunctional Effects, Locking Effects, Sphere Specialties, Technocratic Variants

To make a terrible pun of it, Mage’s Spheres reflect a well-rounded knowledge of nine different yet interrelated elements of reality. In story terms, these nine constructs represent a sort of “unified field theory” of Earthly metaphysics. In game terms, they measure what characters can and cannot do, based upon their understanding of theoretical knowledge and practical results.

---

### The Five Ranks of Sphere Expertise

Each Sphere features five distinct levels of progress and result, represented by the number of dots in your Sphere Trait (see Chapter Two, *Levels of Sphere Progress and Expertise*, p. 68):

1. **• Rank 1: Perception** — The ability to perceive and observe the forces in question. With such basic (yet useful) understanding, a character can sense things that few mortals ever recognize.
2. **•• Rank 2: Manipulation** — The ability to do small things with those forces. At this point, the mage begins to exert minor control over the phenomena she sees, and may use it to work small changes upon herself.
3. **••• Rank 3: Control** — The ability to alter reality in noticeable ways, though these are usually restricted to the mage’s self. In most cases, the Sphere begins inflicting damage at this level, and allows the character to work small alterations on other characters.
4. **•••• Rank 4: Command** — The ability to perform major acts of alteration through the principles of that Sphere. Generally, this level allows the mage to make significant changes to the Patterns of other characters.
5. **••••• Rank 5: Mastery** — The ability to command vast forces in connection with that element of reality. By now, the mage knows almost everything there is to know about the principles of that Sphere and can perform godlike deeds with them.

*(Higher degrees of Sphere mastery might exist beyond 5 dots, but those are rare and optional archmastery traits not explored in the core rules.)*

### The Pattern Spheres

Many entries refer to “the Pattern Spheres” — that is, Spheres that govern physical elements and materials:

* [Forces](mta://spheres/forces) — Physical energies, light, sound, kinetic force, gravity, electricity.
* [Life](mta://spheres/life) — Organic biology, flora, fauna, and human bodies.
* [Matter](mta://spheres/matter) — Inorganic materials, liquids, gases, metals, machines.
* [Spirit](mta://spheres/spirit) — An honorary Pattern Sphere that deals with the metaphysical essence called *ephemera*.

#### Creating from "Nothing"
When a mage creates a physical force, object, or living body from “nothing,” that spell combines a **Pattern Sphere** with the raw creative energy of the [Prime](mta://spheres/prime) Sphere. The Pattern Sphere crafts the form and structure, while the Prime Sphere supplies the Quintessence that makes that form “real.”

### Conjunctional (Combined) Sphere-Effects

Mage’s various Effects come from Spheres; some employ a single Sphere, and others use a combination of Spheres. Sometimes referred to as **conjunctional Effects**, these combinations expand the potential of a single Sphere into Effects that no single Sphere could accomplish on its own.

> **Example:** If Malcolm wants to influence someone’s mood, [Mind 2](mta://spheres/mind) alone will suffice. If he wants to alter a person’s body chemistry to induce euphoria, however, he needs [Life 3](mta://spheres/life) / [Mind 2](mta://spheres/mind)... and if he wants to set it up so that the body chemistry changes several hours later, he must add [Time 4](mta://spheres/time) to that Effect.

---

### Locking an Effect into a Pattern

Normally, a lasting magickal Effect remains in the location where it was originally cast. The mage has essentially transformed that localized reality to suit his desires. Certain Effects travel because of the nature of the things they do (winds blow, fire spreads, shapeshifted mages walk around).

However, if you want to cast a **lingering, mobile Effect** that has been integrated into a specific Pattern and which involves a Sphere not normally related to that Pattern, the Effect must be **“locked”** into the target’s Pattern by a Sphere that does apply:

* To alter someone's mind: use [Mind](mta://spheres/mind).
* To alter her physical body: use [Life](mta://spheres/life).
* To build inorganic material into her body’s Pattern: use both [Life](mta://spheres/life) and [Matter](mta://spheres/matter).
* To make her body invisible by warping light around it: lock the Effect using both [Life](mta://spheres/life) and [Forces](mta://spheres/forces).

> **Important:** Locking an Effect combines the necessary Spheres so that the magick is integrated into a Pattern. Unless the subject of a locked Effect has been turned into a Wonder (see *Crafting Wonders*, pp. 652-653), the Effect still expires when its Duration runs out. **The lock itself does not make an Effect permanent.**

### Sphere Specialties

When your mage accumulates **four dots (Rank 4)** in a Sphere, you can choose a **Sphere Specialty** based upon the things the mage does most often with that Sphere.

* **Rule:** A Sphere specialty allows you to count each **10** you roll as **two successes**, not just one.
* **Additional Specialties:** At the Storyteller’s discretion, additional specialties can be purchased for **4 experience points (XP)** each, assuming the character has spent time and effort training in that specialty.

### Optional Rule: Technocratic Alternative Spheres

Three optional Spheres reflect Technocratic refinements of three mystic Spheres:

1. [Data](mta://spheres/data) — Technocratic analog of [Correspondence](mta://spheres/correspondence) (Virtual Adepts & New World Order).
2. [Primal Utility](mta://spheres/primal_utility) — Hypereconomic application of [Prime](mta://spheres/prime) (The Syndicate).
3. [Dimensional Science](mta://spheres/dimensional_science) — Scientific perspective on [Spirit](mta://spheres/spirit) (Void Engineers).

#### Incompatibility & Mutual Exclusivity
A character **cannot possess both a Technocratic Sphere and the corresponding mystic Sphere** simultaneously. Doing so would reflect a divided perspective on Reality — believing two opposed beliefs about the same thing.

#### Transitioning Between Spheres
A character may transition from one to the other if the story supports it. In such cases:
* The character temporarily loses **1 dot** in that Sphere until she adds **1 point of Arete** (experiencing a revelation that adapts her old expertise to her new understanding).
* At that point, the Sphere returns to its previous rating at no extra experience cost.
