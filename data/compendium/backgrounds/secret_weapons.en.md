### Secret Weapons

You have access to experimental firearms, laboratory prototypes, or lethal weapons of unique design. Whether a directed-energy plasma carbine, a titanium-primium edged blade, or an alchemically tempered rapier, your armaments surprise even seasoned combatants.

In combat, these weapons grant superior damage codes, special fire modes, or bypass mundane body armor. See [Weapons & Combat](mta://weapons/weapons/katana) for combat statistics.

Many secret weapons represent early experimental prototypes of true [Wonders](mta://backgrounds/wonder).
