### Retainers

'What will it be tonight, sir?' When the battle’s over (or shortly before it begins), a faithful retainer can bind your wounds, sharpen your [Weapons](mta://weapons/weapons/katana), clean up the bodies, and make sure you make it to your 8 o’clock morning conference.

Unlike [Allies](mta://backgrounds/allies), retainers are not versed in mystic arts; unlike [Backup](mta://backgrounds/backup), they are not anonymous temps. Each retainer is a devoted servant, executive assistant, mundane apprentice, or loyal construct carrying out your bidding.

He’s your Igor; she’s your Moneypenny. So long as you treat them with basic respect, this Background provides reliable, discreet extra sets of hands.
