### Library

You possess a priceless cache of ancient tomes, leather-bound grimoires, papyrus scrolls, or encrypted drives holding gigabytes of occult secrets and hypertech schematics.

Your library is the cornerstone of arcane research: it reduces difficulties on Enigmas, Occult, and Cosmology rolls, while offering the theoretical basis for inventing new [Rotes](mta://practices/alchemy).

Libraries are frequently pooled by the members of a [Chantry](mta://backgrounds/chantry).

> **Special Rule:** Monumental, Alexandrian archives can scale up to 10 dots. See [Background Rules Over 5](mta://backgrounds/theory_background_rules).
