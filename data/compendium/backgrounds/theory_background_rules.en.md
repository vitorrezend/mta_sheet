M20 Canonical Background Rules:

1. TRAITS OVER FIVE (p. 301):
Normally, human characters cannot purchase Background Traits over five dots. In high-powered elder chronicles, mythic games, or when pooling resources, certain Traits (Allies, Backup, Chantry, Contacts, Cult, Node, Resources, Spies, Wonder) can go up to 10 dots. Ratings beyond five reflect immense global influence, ancient super-structures, or small private armies.

2. POOLING BACKGROUNDS (pp. 301-302):
Mages who share a common bond (a Cabala, a Technocratic Amalgam, or a Chantry) may combine their Background points to build shared assets. Shared Chantries, communal Nodes, joint Libraries, corporate front Resources, and shared Cults are constructed this way. If a member dies or leaves, the cabala loses those contributed points unless experience points are spent to make up the deficit.

3. DIFFERENTIAL BACKGROUNDS (p. 304):
When establishing Alternate Identities, players may link distinct backgrounds (Allies, Resources, Spies) specifically to that fake ID, keeping their primary persona separate.
