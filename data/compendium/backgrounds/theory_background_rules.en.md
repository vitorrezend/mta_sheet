### Traits Over Five (p. 301)

Normally, human characters cannot purchase Background Traits over five dots. In high-powered elder chronicles, mythic games, or when pooling resources, certain Traits can go up to **10 dots**:

- [Allies](mta://backgrounds/allies), [Backup](mta://backgrounds/backup), [Chantry](mta://backgrounds/chantry), [Contacts](mta://backgrounds/contacts) and [Cult](mta://backgrounds/cult)
- [Node](mta://backgrounds/node), [Resources](mta://backgrounds/resources), [Spies](mta://backgrounds/spies) and [Wonder](mta://backgrounds/wonder)

Ratings beyond five reflect immense global influence, ancient super-structures, or small private armies.

### Pooling Backgrounds (pp. 301-302)

Mages who share a common bond (a Cabal, a Technocratic Amalgam, or a Chantry) may combine their Background points to build shared assets. Shared Chantries, communal Nodes, joint Libraries, corporate front Resources, and shared Cults are constructed this way.

> **Losing members:** if a member dies or leaves, the cabal loses those contributed points unless experience points are spent to make up the deficit.

### Differential Backgrounds (p. 304)

When establishing [Alternate Identities](mta://backgrounds/alternate_identity), players may link distinct Backgrounds (Allies, Resources, Spies) specifically to that fake ID, keeping their primary persona separate.
