### Cult

People yearn to believe in something greater than themselves. Whether religious fanatics, New Age seekers, an esoteric lodge, or obsessive fans of a celebrity, you lead a congregation of mortals who revere you, your paradigm, or the divinity you channel.

Unlike [Retainers](mta://backgrounds/retainers) or [Backup](mta://backgrounds/backup), a Cult is driven by spiritual or ideological fervor. Your faithful pray for you, donate funds, construct shrines, and guard your secrets under solemn oaths.

The concentrated faith of a Cult can also be harvested by masters of Prime as worship Quintessence, functioning akin to a living [Node](mta://backgrounds/node).

> **Special Rule:** Massive worldwide cults can scale up to 10 dots in epic chronicles. See [Background Rules Over 5](mta://backgrounds/theory_background_rules).
