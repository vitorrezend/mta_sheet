### Legend

Your reputation resonates in the realm of myth and secret history. Among supernatural courts, occult lodges, and the Deep Umbra, your name invokes reverence, dread, or ancestral devotion.

Unlike [Fame](mta://backgrounds/fame), which is mortal and mundane, Legend operates in the mystic underworld. Spirits, fae, night-creatures, and elder mages recognize your deeds.

Eases diplomacy with mythic entities and reduces difficulties on social rolls when your legendary status is brought to bear. Harmonizes with [Destiny](mta://backgrounds/destiny) and [Blessing](mta://backgrounds/blessing).
