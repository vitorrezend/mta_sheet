### Demesne

Consensus reality dictates that physics must prevail and Paradox must strike the willful mage. However, in special, isolated places, local reality bends entirely to the willworker's paradigm. A Demesne is a physical area — an ancient castle, a consecrated cavern, a forgotten valley, or a hyperdimensional chamber — where Paradox is attenuated and vulgar magick is treated as coincidental within its boundaries!

Unlike a [Sanctum](mta://backgrounds/sanctum), which is a secure workshop against mundanes, a Demesne is a pocket of alternate reality. Within it, a mage may summon firestorms, commune with spirits, or manipulate gravity without standard Paradox backlash.

Most Demesnes are anchored around a potent [Node](mta://backgrounds/node) or nestled in the depths of the Umbra.
