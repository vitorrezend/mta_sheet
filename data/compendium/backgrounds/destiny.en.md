### Destiny

The universe has grand plans for you. You were not chosen by coincidence; your name is etched into the cosmic tapestries of Fate and Time. This grand calling protects you from petty perils so you may meet your predestined culmination.

In game terms, Destiny acts as a dramatic safety net. When final death looms, cosmic forces conspire to intervene (a gun jams, lightning strikes the assailant, a ceiling collapse opens an escape path).

Destiny is a double-edged sword: the higher the rating, the more monumental and tragic your ultimate trial will be. Ties closely with [Blessing](mta://backgrounds/blessing), [Legend](mta://backgrounds/legend), and your [Avatar](mta://backgrounds/avatar).
