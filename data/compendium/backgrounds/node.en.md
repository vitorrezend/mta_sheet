### Node / Requisition Pool

Reality pulses with primal energy at mystical junctures where dragon tracks and ley lines intersect. A Node is a tangible source of free Quintessence and Tass (the condensed physical form of Quintessence, such as glowing fungi, quartz crystals, or enchanted honey).

For the Technocratic Union, Nodes are zero-point generators, geothermal sub-stations, or Requisition Pools of liquid Primium.

A mage with Node dots harvests a periodic yield of Quintessence to fuel spells and empower [Wonders](mta://backgrounds/wonder). Nodes are the most coveted prize in the World of Darkness, defended within fortified [Chantries](mta://backgrounds/chantry).

> **Special Rule:** Legendary nexus points can reach up to 10 dots. See [Background Rules Over 5](mta://backgrounds/theory_background_rules).
