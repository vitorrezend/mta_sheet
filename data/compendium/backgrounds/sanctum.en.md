### Sanctum / Laboratory

Every willworker needs a haven where they can shut the door on the mundane world and conduct operations without interruption. Whether an attic occult studio, a basement workshop, a sterile cleanroom, or an isolated observatory, your Sanctum is your private workshop.

Within your Sanctum, experimental arts and rites using [Instruments and Focus](mta://instruments/tools_of_focus) take place in a secure, controlled setting free from mundane eavesdropping.

Many mages establish their personal Sanctums within the defended perimeter of a [Chantry](mta://backgrounds/chantry) or atop a [Demesne](mta://backgrounds/demesne).
