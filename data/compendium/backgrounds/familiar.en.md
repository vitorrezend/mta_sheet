### Familiar / Companion

A spirit companion, mystical beast, or sentient bioconstruct has bound its existence to your mage. A familiar is no mere pet or servant; it possesses awakened sentience, its own intellect, and a profound spiritual bond with the willworker's soul.

The familiar can store Quintessence in its pattern, share senses with the mage, and even absorb Paradox points to shield its master from catastrophic backlashes!

> **Classic Rulebook Example (M20, p. 313):**
> Say that Spider Chase has a Familiar 3 blue tarantula. The spider can store up to 3 Quintessence per dot and absorb up to 5 Paradox per dot in the Background (a total of 15 Paradox for Spider's spider).
> Should the familiar reach maximum Paradox capacity, the whole amount explodes in a violent backlash or the animal is obliterated into the Umbra! Protecting your familiar is as crucial as guarding your own soul.

Ties deeply to [Avatar](mta://backgrounds/avatar) and [Node](mta://backgrounds/node).
