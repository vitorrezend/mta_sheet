### Totem

A Great Spirit of the Umbra — Bear, Raven, Wolf, the North Wind, or the Machine God — has adopted you or your cabal. The Totem bestows spiritual blessings, bonus dice on specific pools, and mystical boons.

In return, the Totem’s followers must uphold a strict ban or taboo (never telling a lie, never spilling wolf blood, or planting a tree each new moon).

Harmonizes deeply with [Avatar](mta://backgrounds/avatar) and [Blessing](mta://backgrounds/blessing).
