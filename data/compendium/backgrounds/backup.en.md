### Backup

You can call in the cavalry and expect help when needed… in a limited capacity, anyhow. Thanks to your membership in some organization, you’re able to request Backup and have a small team of useful folks swoop in and take care of business. Unlike [Allies](mta://backgrounds/allies), these people are largely faceless, have limited skills, and are more or less expendable.

Backup characters come from a large pool of skilled but unAwakened personnel. Their relationship to the player characters is pretty much a matter of convenience, with no special loyalty attached. Though they might risk their lives on the mage’s behalf, the help’s not personal. These folks are just doing their job.

Backup tends to arrive as a pack of gun-wielding grunts who rush in to cover a mage’s escape, clean up scenes, or provide logistical cover. For celebrities, it may be security and roadies; for scholars, research assistants; for scientists, lab techs and repair units.

> **Special Rule:** This Background may reach up to 10 dots in mythic or high-tier chronicles. See [Background Rules Over 5](mta://backgrounds/theory_background_rules).
