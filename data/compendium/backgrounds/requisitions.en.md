### Requisitions

In structured organizations like the Technocratic Union or the Order of Hermes, you don't need to purchase heavy hardware out of your own wallet. With this Background, you can requisition field gear, armored vehicles, [Weapons](mta://weapons/weapons/katana), or specialized tech directly from quartermasters for the duration of an assigned mission.

> **Canonical Requisition Difficulty Chart (M20, p. 316):**
> - Pet / Superior's Favorite: Difficulty 4
> - Respectful / Good Standing: Difficulty 5
> - Neutral / Bureaucratic: Difficulty 6
> - Under Scrutiny / Disliked: Difficulty 7
> - Despised / Blacklisted: Difficulty 8

Gear must be returned in working condition once the operation concludes. Damaging official assets results in severe disciplinary review.

> **Special Rule:** Can reach up to 10 dots for special ops. See [Background Rules Over 5](mta://backgrounds/theory_background_rules).
