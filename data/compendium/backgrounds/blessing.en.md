### Blessing

Someone out there has left its mark on you. That mark is usually beneficial, but it comes with uncanny side effects. Maybe you always find a few extra bucks when you need them but get stuck with the check when you grab drinks with friends. This Background gives you a minor perk at the cost of a related quirk.

In game terms, Blessings gives you a single, minor, story-based benefit tied into the beliefs that focus your magick. An Akashic might be blessed by wind-god Fei Lian with a permanent gentle breeze; an Amazon never gets lost (blessing of Athena); a one-eyed Syndicate executive has strange insights and a painting of two ravens hinting at Odin.

This Background ties neatly with [Destiny](mta://backgrounds/destiny), [Legend](mta://backgrounds/legend), [Past Lives](mta://backgrounds/past_lives), and [Totem](mta://backgrounds/totem).

> **Game System:** Choose one non-combat, story-based ability. In high-stakes situations requiring a roll, the Storyteller deducts one level of difficulty per dot in this Background (e.g., Blessings 4 grants -4 difficulty). Does not affect combat or Arete magick casting rolls.
