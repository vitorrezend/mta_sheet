### Fame

Your face is recognized in streets, television, social media, and stages worldwide. You are a celebrity: a rock star, a digital influencer with millions of followers, a bestselling author, an acclaimed scientist, or a championship athlete.

Fame dramatically eases entry into exclusive circles, meetings with authorities, and public favors. However, it is the mortal enemy of stealth: paparazzi, obsessive fans, and bloggers track your movements, making covert operations and [Arcane](mta://backgrounds/arcane) nearly impossible without heavy disguise.

Harmonizes naturally with [Contacts](mta://backgrounds/contacts), [Influence](mta://backgrounds/influence), and [Resources](mta://backgrounds/resources).
