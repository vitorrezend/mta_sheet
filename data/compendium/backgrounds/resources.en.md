### Resources

As any Syndicate rep could tell you, there’s good money to be made with magick – very good money if you play your cards right. Naturally, the Syndicate won’t refer to what they do as magick – it’s simply the Art of Desire / [Hypereconomics](mta://practices/hypereconomics), honed to a science. Whatever you choose to call it, this Background means you’ve got cash and goods on hand… at upper levels, lots of cash and goods.

Given the staggering wealth that certain mages can accrue, this Background goes to 10. Such wealth isn’t just the dominion of the Syndicate: Hermetic wizards are infamous for luxuries, Choristers command vast foundations, and Ecstatic rock stars make fortunes.

Transactions these days are conducted in virtual money: debit cards, credit cards, online deposits. Throwing around bags of cash or transmuting lead into gold is too suspicious and draws intense scrutiny. A wise willworker attracts capital using [Money and Wealth](mta://instruments/money_wealth) as a focus of power.

> **Special Rule:** Multi-billionaire tiers from 6 to 10 dots may be restricted by the Storyteller. See [Background Rules Over 5](mta://backgrounds/theory_background_rules).
