### Contacts

Information makes the modern world spin, and having the right ears in the right places is worth as much as packing heat. With this Background, you maintain a web of acquaintances, colleagues, city clerks, journalists, or street people who can feed you gossip, confidential tips, and early warnings.

Unlike [Allies](mta://backgrounds/allies), your contacts won't get into shootouts for you or quit their jobs to help; and unlike [Spies](mta://backgrounds/spies), they do not run active intelligence operations on your behalf. They are everyday folks whom you buy lunch, do mutual favors for, or keep on friendly terms.

Each dot in Contacts gives you one major contact (a reliable source in a key sector like police forensics or a newspaper desk) plus a broad ring of minor contacts (clerks, doormen, cabbies, receptionists).

> **Special Rule:** This Background can go up to 10 dots for international intelligence webs. See [Background Rules Over 5](mta://backgrounds/theory_background_rules).
