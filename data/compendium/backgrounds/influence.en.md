### Influence

You pull the strings of political, corporate, legal, or media power across a city or region. With a discrete phone call or closed-door conference, you can divert police inquiries, expedite court injunctions, or sway city council votes.

Unlike [Resources](mta://backgrounds/resources), which measures liquid financial wealth, Influence reflects institutional sway and owed favors from prominent public officials.

> **Special Rule:** In elder or global chronicles, Influence can reach up to 10 dots, commanding entire metropolises or continental industries. See [Background Rules Over 5](mta://backgrounds/theory_background_rules).
