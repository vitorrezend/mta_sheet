### Wonder / Device, Invention

Objects that defy conventional physical laws. For traditional willworkers, they are enchanted Talismans, rings of power, arcane blades, or cloaks of elven stealth; for the Technocracy, they are hypertech Devices, quantum microcomputers, or repulsor drones.

A Wonder stores its own pool of Quintessence, contains bound Sphere effects, and generates no Paradox on its own, allowing the bearer to unleash prodigies without burning personal Willpower.

Wonders are frequently recharged at a [Node](mta://backgrounds/node) and complement [Secret Weapons](mta://backgrounds/secret_weapons).
