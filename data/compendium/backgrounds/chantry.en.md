### Chantry / Construct

In the hazardous world of the Awakened, it’s always a good idea to have some like-minded associates and a stronghold with some fair security. Since the Golden Age of Wizardry, such places have been called Covenants: centers where a group of allied mages stake out territory, consolidate resources, and agreed to assist and defend one another. In later years, the Order of Reason adopted that idea and built the Technocratic Union around that principle. By the 21st century, Tradition mages call those places Chantries, the Union calls them Constructs, and other mages call them temples, lodges, or mosques.

In game terms, a Chantry or Construct is a base of operations. A character with this Background belongs to such a base and has certain benefits from, and responsibilities to, the group that maintains that base. Most Technocratic agents belong to a Construct by default. Mystic mages have more of a choice, but many belong to Chantries as a matter of practicality. There is strength in numbers, after all.

Beginning characters start as low-ranking members of an established stronghold. By pooling points among members, a cabal can assemble the resources necessary to build a place of its own, constructing shared assets like [Nodes](mta://backgrounds/node), [Libraries](mta://backgrounds/library), and [Sanctums](mta://backgrounds/sanctum).

> **Special Rule:** This Background can go up to 10 dots via pooled cabal assets. See [Background Rules Over 5](mta://backgrounds/theory_background_rules).
