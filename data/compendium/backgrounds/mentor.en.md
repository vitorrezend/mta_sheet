### Mentor

Someone older, wiser, and more experienced on the Path of Magick has taken you under their wing. Whether an elder Hermetic archmage, an Akashic zen master, a Technocratic supervisor, or an elder shaman, your mentor offers counsel in dark hours, defends your name in tribunals, and unlocks advanced Sphere knowledge and [Libraries](mta://backgrounds/library).

Mentors demand respect, loyalty, and arduous chores. They won't fight your battles for you — a true master expects you to learn from your own scars.

Frequently anchored to a [Chantry](mta://backgrounds/chantry).
