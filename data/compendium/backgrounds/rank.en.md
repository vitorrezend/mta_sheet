### Rank

You hold an official commissioned standing within the rigid hierarchy of the Technocratic Union, a military outfit, a corporate cartel, or a structured Hermetic house.

Your rank commands obedience from subordinates, unlocks access to classified compounds, and authorizes [Backup](mta://backgrounds/backup) and [Requisitions](mta://backgrounds/requisitions).

However, higher rank brings heavier accountability and relentless scrutiny from upper management!
