### Water Elemental

> **Source:** M20 Core Rulebook, Appendix I: Allies and Antagonists, p. 636

Often, but not always, embodied as a seductive young woman, the water elemental’s smooth voice and sinuous form recall the mermaids and sirens inspired by the sea. Her forms echo the nature of the water from which she comes: a swamp hag, spring child, river nymph, or oozing sludge girl.

- **Traits:** Willpower 6, Rage 4, Gnosis 8, Essence 30
- **Charms:** Blast, Cleanse the Blight, Flood, Healing, Materialize
