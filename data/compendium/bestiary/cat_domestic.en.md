### Cat, Domestic-Size

> **Source:** M20 Core Rulebook, Appendix I: Allies and Antagonists, p. 619

- **Abilities:** Alertness 3, Athletics (Climbing) 4, Awareness 2, Brawl 2, Empathy 3, Intimidation 1, Stealth 4
