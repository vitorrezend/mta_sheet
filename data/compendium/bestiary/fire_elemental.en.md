### Fire Elemental

> **Source:** M20 Core Rulebook, Appendix I: Allies and Antagonists, p. 636

Hungry and crackling, the genderless fire spirit consumes everything that will burn. If happy, this entity remains a small glowing ember speaking in puffs of smoke; outraged, it explodes into a howling conflagration, igniting everything in its path.

- **Traits:** Willpower 5, Rage 10, Gnosis 5, Essence 30
- **Charms:** Blast, Create Fire, Materialize
