### Monkey Spirit

> **Source:** M20 Core Rulebook, Appendix I: Allies and Antagonists, p. 633

- **Traits:** Willpower 7, Rage 9, Gnosis 7, Essence 25 (Gafflings) to 50 (Jagglings)
- **Charms:** Influence, Materialize, Shapeshift. Some monkey spirits can also Armor up, Spirit Away, and evoke an Umbraquake.
- **Powers:** The ability to craft or destroy solid elements (Matter 3). Monkey Jagglings may also sidestep distances (Correspondence 3), “monkey” with probability (Entropy 2), or heal or inflict massive injuries (Life 3).
