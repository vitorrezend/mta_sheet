### Owl Spirit

> **Source:** M20 Core Rulebook, Appendix I: Allies and Antagonists, p. 633

- **Traits:** Willpower 7, Rage 4, Gnosis 6, Essence 20 (Gaffling) to 40 (Jaggling)
- **Charms:** Influence, Materialize, Tracking. Certain owl spirits can also Create Wind, Cleanse the Blight, and employ Corruption.
- **Powers:** Foresight (Time 3). Owl Jagglings may manipulate fate and fortune, command decay (Entropy 2 or Entropy 4), or possess people and walk among their dreams (Mind 4).
