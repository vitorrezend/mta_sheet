### Electricity Elemental

> **Source:** M20 Core Rulebook, Appendix I: Allies and Antagonists, p. 636

Impersonal and fearsome, this bright snapping figure flashes like oddly human lightning, arcing between conductive materials and sizzling everything that can burn. If such an elemental speaks, its words buzz like furious metal bees.

- **Traits:** Willpower 6, Rage 7, Gnosis 5, Essence 40
- **Charms:** Control Electrical Systems, Lightning Bolts, Short Out
