### Horse Spirit

> **Source:** M20 Core Rulebook, Appendix I: Allies and Antagonists, pp. 632-633

- **Traits:** Willpower 9, Rage 7, Gnosis 7, Essence 25 (Gaffling) to 50 (Jaggling)
- **Charms:** Armor, Materialize. Some horse spirits can also Create Wind, call Lightning Bolts, Spirit Away, and stomp up an Umbraquake.
- **Powers:** Incredible speed (increased movement) and the ability to cross vast distances (Correspondence 3). Horse Jagglings can also co-locate (Correspondence 4), fly (Forces 3), or summon storms (Forces 4/ Prime 2).
