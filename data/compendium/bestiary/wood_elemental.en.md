### Wood Elemental

> **Source:** M20 Core Rulebook, Appendix I: Allies and Antagonists, p. 637

Shaped like a skeletal (though perhaps quite lovely) woman, the wood spirit evokes the fabled dryad no matter where she happens to be from. Certain wood spirits resemble horned men, too: naked, rustic figures with ruddy skins and often leafy faces, darting between trees. Such entities almost never speak; if they do, their words whisper like the breeze between branches or clack with hollow resonance.

- **Traits:** Willpower 4, Rage 4, Gnosis 5, Essence 25
- **Charms:** Cleanse the Blight, Element Sense (within wood), Materialize, Shapeshift, Soul Reading
