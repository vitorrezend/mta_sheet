### Leopard, Jaguar, Panther, or Cougar

> **Source:** M20 Core Rulebook, Appendix I: Allies and Antagonists, p. 619

- **Attributes:** Strength 4, Dexterity 3, Stamina 3, Perception 3, Intelligence 2, Wits 3
- **Abilities:** Alertness 3, Athletics 3, Awareness 3, Brawl 3, Empathy 2, Intimidation 2, Stealth 4
- **Willpower:** 4
- **Health Levels:** OK, -1, -1, -2, -2, -5, Incapacitated
- **Armor Rating:** 0 (three soak dice, total)
- **Attack:** Bite for five dice; claw for five dice (all lethal).
