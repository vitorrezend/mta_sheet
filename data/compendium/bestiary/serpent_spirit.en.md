### Serpent Spirit

> **Source:** M20 Core Rulebook, Appendix I: Allies and Antagonists, p. 632

- **Traits:** Willpower 5, Rage 6, Gnosis 8, Essence 20 (Gaffling) to 30 (Jaggling)
- **Charms:** Blighted Touch, Cleanse the Blight, Influence; some serpent spirits also have Materialize, Possession, and Shapeshift.
- **Powers:** Regeneration, Healing, and Injury (Life 2 and 3). Serpent Jagglings can send and enter dreams (Mind 3 and 4), locate and tap Quintessence (Prime 3), or control fate and fortune (Entropy 2).
