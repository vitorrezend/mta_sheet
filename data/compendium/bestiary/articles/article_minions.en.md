> **Source:** M20 Core Rulebook, Appendix I: Allies and Antagonists, p. 638

Servitors of greater powers or principles, Minions display an intellect of sorts. Some of them follow an instinctive purpose with only the faintest glimmer of sentience. Others can be as cunning and perceptive as any mortal human, though perhaps not cosmically aware like the greater spirit entities.
