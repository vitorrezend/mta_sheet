### Earth Elemental

> **Source:** M20 Core Rulebook, Appendix I: Allies and Antagonists, p. 636

Composed of lumpy dirt and stone, this vaguely masculine elemental speaks in a grating voice and refuses to hurry along. Like the earth itself, he does what he does in his own sweet time.

- **Traits:** Willpower 10, Rage 8, Gnosis 5, Essence 40
- **Charms:** Armor, Materialize, Stone Fists (as Blast, but hurls stone instead), Shapeshift, Umbraquake
